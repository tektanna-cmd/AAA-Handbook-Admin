How to update the Android app

  1. Open a school profile, policy section, timetable entry or notice.
  2. Choose Edit, update text or timetable fields, and Save draft.
  3. Submit for review; the Principal/authorized Publisher checks the changes.
  4. Publisher presses Approve & publish. The public Firestore copy is replaced.
  5. The Android app checks for new approved content when opened or when readers refresh; a local offline copy is retained.

Deleting: Use Archive to remove content from future synchronization. The public copy is removed after approval of the archive. An existing offline copy may remain on phones until their next sync.

Photos: Current no-billing mode uses a verified public HTTPS image URL; do not upload private drafts directly to public GitHub Pages.
